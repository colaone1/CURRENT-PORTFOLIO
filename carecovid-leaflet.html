CARECOVID Leaflet Design

Print and digital marketing material with a clear information hierarchy.

Project Overview

Designed an informative leaflet for CARECOVID that communicates healthcare services to patients and stakeholders. The layout balances a professional medical look with a clear reading order for print and digital distribution.

Skills and tools

Layout Design Print Design Typography Adobe InDesign Adobe Illustrator Brand Integration

Design Process

Content, layout, and production

  • Mapped key messages and audience needs before locking the layout
  • Used a grid to organise information while keeping brand consistency
  • Applied type, colour, and imagery for a professional medical aesthetic
  • Prepared files for print, including colour and resolution checks

Design Features

Information design

  • Clear hierarchy so important information is easy to find
  • Type sizes chosen for readability across audiences

Brand and print

  • CARECOVID logo and brand elements used consistently
  • Optimised for print production and digital sharing
