CARECOVID Logo Design

Professional brand identity and logo design for the healthcare sector.

Project Overview

Designed a modern, professional logo for CARECOVID, focusing on a brand identity that conveys trust, care, and medical expertise. The design uses clean typography and strategic colour choices for a strong visual presence in healthcare.

Skills and tools

Logo Design Brand Identity Typography Color Theory Adobe Illustrator Adobe Photoshop

Design Process

Research and refinement

  • Researched healthcare branding and competitor logos to find a clear point of difference
  • Developed multiple concepts exploring type, colour, and visual marks
  • Refined spacing, contrast, and scalability for print and digital use
  • Prepared the final logo in formats that stay clear at small and large sizes

Design Features

Typography and colour

  • Clean, readable type for professional healthcare use
  • Colour choices that support trust, care, and contrast

Application

  • Vector artwork that stays sharp at any size
  • Works in colour and monochrome across media
